D-253 (PROPOSAL) evidence: how the gate's machine (QEMU q35 + OVMF) routes PCI INTx.
Captured 2026-11-14 by the drivers task, run 10.

Method: qemu-system-x86_64 8.2.2 (Debian 1:8.2.2+ds-0ubuntu1.18) -machine q35 -m 256, OVMF_CODE_4M.fd (Debian ovmf),
a virtio-blk FAT disk at 00:02, e1000 at 00:03, rtl8139 at 00:04 (romfile= empty),
booted to OVMF's internal UEFI shell, startup.nsh: 'dmem' + 'memmap' (table pointers,
the ACPI reclaim range), then 'dmem F76D000 12000' (all of ACPI_Recl, 18 pages) and
'dmem E0000 20000' + 'dmem 9FC00 400' (BIOS area, EBDA). The hex dump was turned back
into bytes, the DSDT cut out by its length (8424 bytes, checksum 0) and disassembled
with iasl -d (version 20230628).
